Plan only up close

Zoomed in (default): calendar, a short picture of this week, and today's plan below. Zoom out and the plan steps away: the sheet becomes just the calendar on top and the picture filling everything below it. Tap any day in the calendar (or +) and you drop back in to that day's plan.

Rules for every option
  • Close (default): Rezzy on the path, this week's days as stones (done / missed / ahead). One line: "This week · 3 of 4 days done".
  • Middle: the months around you as markers. "Week 6 of 25".
  • Whole: start to goal, the date, and one number: "136 days to the summit".
  • No phases. We don't know when a student moves from learning to revising, so the path only shows facts: days, months, the exam date.
  • Range, month or no date: the goal sits in fog or cloud until the date is exact.
What's live and what's new
The sheet, calendar and pill come from the new-nav prototype (Exam calendar v10 / app-prototype). The day plan below uses live components (WeekDayDetail rows, the study-time row, cta-glossy, SF Pro Rounded, the 3D icons). NEW is only the timeline module.
