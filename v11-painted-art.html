The path is home

From the Oct 23 sync. Opening the app shows your path, zoomed in on this week: Rezzy, your sessions as stones, and the next session's 3 activities. Pinch, scroll or use Today · Week · Journey to pull back to the whole way to the exam. The plan is weekly, at your own pace: sessions aren't tied to days, so nothing is "missed". The calendar only looks back (top-right button, or tap a done stone).

What changed from v9
  • Path first. v9 hid the path inside the calendar sheet, behind a pill. Now it's the home screen, and you zoom into what to do.
  • Sessions, not days. This week's target (e.g. 3 sessions) sits on the path. Done ones stay where you did them; the rest wait ahead of Rezzy, any day this week. Daily students see "Today's session".
  • Calendar = the past. Rings for what you studied, a row per week. No future days with plans in them.
  • Same three distances: Today (Rezzy + next session) → Week (sessions, past weeks summed up) → Journey (months, the exam, one number).
Rules
  • One number per level: "Session 2 · 37 min", "1 of 3 sessions", "136 days to the summit".
  • No Learn/Practise/Revise labels. Session names come from order in the week (start, practise, wrap up).
  • Unsure exam date: fog on the goal, as in v9.
  • Art is a placeholder for the illustrator.
